# Rillet overview

Rillet is a wall-mounted irrigation controller for home gardens. It comes in two
models: Rillet Lite drives 2 zones and Rillet Pro drives 6 zones.

Each zone delivers up to 0.5 L/s.

Typical power draw is 40 W. The supply is rated 0.12 kW.

Component cost is about $40 per unit.
