# Rillet app

The home screen shows each zone's name and whether it is watering, from the `zones`
field of the status message.

The maintenance screen shows the pump's lifetime run hours, from the `pump_hours` field
of the status message.

The about screen shows the unit's serial number, from the `serial_no` field of the status
message.

The app follows the phone's dark mode setting.
